Plan

Roadmap

1

Passive CA1: leak, capacitance, axial coupling

Validated

Transfer ratio and impedance match the analytic solution to within 0.01 MΩ. The response is low-pass only, as RC theory requires.

2

Active dendrite: voltage-dependent Ih

Current priority

Lowet kinetics in place; a peak appears at 2.3 Hz. The frozen-h control separates kinetics from conductance. Next: τh × ḡh map and Ceballos parameters.

3

Stochastic and oscillatory inputs

Partial

8 vs 40 Hz × Ih × noise with fixed seeds. σ = 0.01 nA·ms½ is too weak to change anything; a σ sweep is needed.

4

Spike dynamics: threshold, adaptation, bursting

Stub

The LIF soma phase-locks (PPC 0.43 at 8 Hz, 0.98 at 40 Hz) but cannot burst. Next rung: AdEx, or somatic IM + INaP.

5

CA3 → CA1 microcircuit, AIS

Later

Separate CA3 model, AIS compartment, CA3→CA1 synapse, spatially modulated input.
