hippsim 0.14 · lab simulator

Simulate real neurons, and know where every number comes from.

hippsim runs published neuron models on reconstructed cells, right in the browser. Pick a cell, run its baseline, change anything, and compare. Every value is tied to its source, and the solver is checked against NEURON and Brian2.

What is measured and what is modelled

Under every simulation, five rows keep these apart:

  • Experimental morphologythe reconstruction itself: who traced it, where it is archived
  • Published modelchannel equations and densities, each with its paper; unknown sources say unspecified
  • Adapted herehow a model was applied to this cell, and what you changed
  • Tested computationallythe test, every value, the assumptions
  • Validatedwhat has been checked (NEURON, Brian2) and what has not (recordings)
