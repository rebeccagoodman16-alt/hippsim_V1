About & help

hippsim runs real reconstructed neurons and published models in the browser, with every starting value, manipulation and source on record. Version 0.15 · 13 Nov 2026. Full documentation: Docs (models, equations, validation, citing).

How to use

  1. Neurons (home): pick a cell, rotate it, press Simulate to inject current and watch the voltage spread. Save the run or download the same model as a NEURON or Brian2 script.
  2. Tools: the model library (channels, synapses, circuits, plasticity), each with literature baselines and perturbations.
  3. In a tool, Baseline: the starting values, the model they come from, and its references.
  4. Run, then Change one thing: move a control, or apply a published manipulation (drug, disease model, genotype), and run again. The bar shows how many values differ from baseline; Reset undoes them.
  5. Save run: stored online with every value and the plots. Runs reopens it exactly, sorts runs into folders, and downloads a citable zip.

Legend

Apical dendrite
Dendrites on the apical side of the soma (toward stratum radiatum / lacunosum-moleculare in CA1–CA3).
Basal dendrite
Basal dendrites, and all dendrites of cells without an apical tree (granule cells, interneurons).
Axon
Traced axon. Axons added by modellers are hidden and say so on the cell's page.
Lab
A model implemented and tested in the lab, as opposed to a published model re-implemented.
NEURON check
Passive cable model in NEURON (Rm 20 kΩ·cm², Ra 150 Ω·cm, Cm 1 µF/cm²): soma input resistance and the length constant that best fits attenuation along the dendrites at 0 and 100 Hz.
Validated
Each tool's check against its source is on Models → Validation.

Data & sources

87 reconstructions from NeuroMorpho.Org, ModelDB, Janelia (MouseLight, hemibrain, MANC), FANC and the Allen Cell Types Database; each cell's page names its archive record and paper. Models follow 46 papers; DOIs were checked against Crossref and OpenAlex. Corrections to inherited data: doi_corrections.json. Follow each archive's citation policy when you reuse a cell.

Cite

Cite the papers behind what you used (Baseline card, cell page, or the references.bib in a run's zip) and hippsim itself (CITATION.cff). NeuroMorpho.Org cells: also Ascoli et al. (2007) J Neurosci 27:9247.

Your data

Saved runs are stored with your account and are visible to people the simulator is shared with; only you can change or delete yours. Display settings (size, colours, layout) stay on this device.

Release notes

0.15 · 13 Nov 2026

  • hippsim vs NEURON, explained: the remaining differences come from two geometry conventions that only matter together: NEURON's pipeline drops points that repeat their parent (many reconstructions repeat a soma point as the first point of every dendrite), and starts soma-attached dendrites at their first point. With both, hippsim matches NEURON's input resistance and, in most cells, its spike count; each cell's comparison now includes this combined run and R_in for each diagnostic. hippsim's defaults are unchanged.
  • Four more questions: recording temperature, NMDA dependence, Aβ-like A-type loss during θ input, and weakened inhibition in a network (12 in all).
  • First-time visitors start Simulate from a question; the choice (question / choose myself) is remembered.

0.14 · 5 Nov 2026

  • Start from a question: eight biological questions (excitability, Iₕ, resonance, back-propagation, distal input, Aβ-like A-type loss, bursting, feedforward inhibition). Each picks the neuron and the model, says why, gives the wet-lab equivalent and what to expect (with its sources), runs it, explains the result in plain terms, and applies the drug in one click to compare. On the Neurons page, in Simulate, and as a Docs page.
  • App menu inside a neuron: a Menu handle on the bottom bar pulls up Neurons · Runs · Tools · Models · Docs (or hover the bottom edge, or press M).
  • How hippsim works: an intro page, shown once on the first visit and linked from the Neurons page and footer.
  • Docs ↔ app links: every model and test in the docs opens in the app; the app links back to the model's docs.
  • Repository files (YAML, JSON, Markdown, CSV) open inside the app with Back; the installed app offers a Reload when a new version is ready (Display → Check for updates).

0.13 · 5 Nov 2026

  • Where this comes from: five rows under every simulation separate what is measured from what is modelled: experimental morphology (identity, archive, reconstruction ID, papers, how it was traced, and what hippsim estimated or changed), the published model (each mechanism with its original reference, passive values with sources, channel distribution across soma, AIS, axon, basal, trunk, obliques and tuft), what was adapted for this cell, what is being tested (every value and assumption) and what has been validated. Unknown sources are labelled *unspecified*.
  • The Migliore option is now Migliore-derived CA1 channel model; on any cell other than a CA1 pyramidal cell it is flagged as a CA1-derived parameterization that is not validated for that cell type.
  • Show explanations on hover (Display): models, tests, channels, sliders, branch tools and cell parts explain themselves on hover or tap. Cards are short either way; Details holds the full text.
  • hippsim vs NEURON, in detail: for every cell, the same Hodgkin–Huxley model in both simulators with traces at the soma, AIS and two dendritic sites, rest / peak / spikes / latency / threshold / max dV/dt, Na⁺ and K⁺ currents, set-up equivalence, membrane area and compartments, passive R_in and τ, where the traces diverge, and one-change-at-a-time diagnostics (tools/compare_engines.py). Nothing is tuned. Finding for CA3 l24b (hippsim 0 spikes, NEURON 1): the soma–dendrite junction convention, not the channel equations; documented on the Validation page. hippsim's default is unchanged.

0.10 · 5 Nov 2026

  • Simulate is one panel, beside the 3D cell: choose the scale, the model and the simulator, run the baseline, then manipulate it. Nothing opens another page. - *Single cell*: Hodgkin–Huxley (runs in hippsim, NEURON or Brian2), Migliore 2005 CA1 channels (the Neuron lab's model), and your research model (Golomb I_M + I_NaP on the Migliore dendrites, θ + γ input; CA1 pyramidal cells). - *Circuit*: this cell with θ-modulated excitatory synapses on its dendrites and feedforward inhibition at the soma. - *Population*: Brunel (2000) E–I network of 5 000 integrate-and-fire neurons, with its four parameter sets. - Every model shows its baseline values and sources, offers drug and model manipulations, and draws each run over its baseline.
