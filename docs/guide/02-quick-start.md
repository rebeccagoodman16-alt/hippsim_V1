---
title: Quick start
---
From opening hippsim to a saved, citable run: six steps, nothing to install.

1. **Neurons** (home page): pick a region or type a cell name (CA1, granule, n123).
2. The cell opens in 3D. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, hover or tap a branch for its compartment and distance from the soma. **Details** shows lengths, diameters, branching and a Sholl profile.
3. Press **Simulate**. Choose the scale (single cell, circuit, population), the model and where it runs (hippsim, NEURON, Brian2).
4. **Run baseline**. Read the trace, the result and the baseline values with their sources; the voltage plays across the cell.
5. **Manipulate the baseline**: a drug button (TTX, TEA, 4-AP, ZD7288, XE991, bicuculline, APV, NBQX), a slider, or tap a branch to move the electrode. **Run**: the new trace is drawn over the baseline.
6. **Save run**. It appears in **Runs** and reopens into the same cell, model and settings.

**Keyboard** in the 3D view: space = rotate, 0 = front view, Esc = back to all neurons.
**Display** (top right): text size, colours (incl. high contrast, colour-blind safe), light or dark, web or phone view.
